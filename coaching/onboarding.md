# Accelerator onboarding: enrollment through Week 1

Built from what worked with three of Jaz's most successful clients: Aliah (5540 W Thompson), Kim Holloway (1336 N Hobart, 2427 W Oakdale) and Impact Plus Holdings (2512 N Gratz → 4–5 rentals). It also fixes what slowed each of them down. Every email below is a draft for Jaz to review. Nothing is sent automatically.

## The pattern that worked

Across all three clients, Jaz did the same three things at every step:

1. **A Loom video** walking through the numbers or the document.
2. **A short "I will need the following" list**, grouped by who needs it (lender, title, property).
3. **One clear next action with a deadline.**

And after every call, she sent a recap: what we decided, the client's worry and the answer, a win, the homework with due dates, and what to bring next time.

## Intake packet (due 2 days before kickoff)

From Impact Plus Holdings' "Next Steps" email, which got the fastest start:

- Money Map, filled in, with a short Loom video of the client walking through it
- Credit monitoring set up (and credit unfrozen)
- Business documents, if they have a company already: certificate of organization, operating agreement, tax ID number letter, certificate of good standing
- The last 2 years of tax returns and W-2s or 1099s
- The last 3 months of bank statements, personal and business
- Most recent pay stub
- Driver's license
- Mission statement workbook (feeds their website)

Send scans, never phone photos. Use a scanner app.

## Website track

We build each client a website on GoHighLevel so their company looks established to lenders, contractors and sellers. The brand kit comes from their mission statement.

1. **Week 1:** the client finishes the mission statement workbook.
2. **Week 2:** we create the brand kit from it: name and tagline, colors, fonts, logo direction and voice. The client approves it.
3. **Weeks 2–3:** we build the site in GoHighLevel from the brand kit.
4. **Before the first offer (Week 10):** the site is live.

## Guardrails learned from these files

Each one goes into the week where it would have saved time:

| Lesson | Where it came from | Week |
|---|---|---|
| Form the company in the right state and get every document **before** offers | Aliah's certificate arrived 3 days before closing | 1 |
| Company name exactly the same everywhere | Title came back under the wrong company name | 1 |
| Cash plan includes reserves and escrow for taxes and insurance | Kim's 424 N 64th loan was denied when escrow left her short | 5 |
| A lender **and** a backup lined up before the offer | Thompson and Colorado slipped waiting on lender pricing | 5 |
| Write down the client's area preferences, and send only matching listings | Kim had said no more West Philadelphia | 2 |
| Set the maximum price before offering | Kim's Hobart offer: "stand firm at 90k knowing the max is 92k" | 10 |
| Order the title and lien search the day the contract is signed | Albanus (second mortgage, judgment) and Thompson (seller's open mortgage) | 10 |
| Bring in specialists before the inspection deadline, and plan to renegotiate | Thompson: price renegotiated using the 65% rule after structural findings | 11 |
| Insurance binder ordered at contract: replacement cost, mortgagee clause, who pays | Albanus binder arrived the morning of closing | 11 |
| Draw template and lender's draw rules in writing before closing | Thompson draw disputes | 12 |
| Rental license, rental suitability certificate and lead-safe certificate **before** listing | Hobart sat empty about 6 months | After closing |
| Calendar the hard money due date; refinance credit check 90 days before | Colorado and Albanus extension fees; a late payment hurt credit before the refinance | After closing |
| After the refinance: door number two review | Impact Plus went from 1 rental to 5 this way | After closing |

## Fix the Roadmap to Closing template

The version sent to all three clients needs four changes:

1. Add: "Before you send any money, call the title company at a number you found yourself to confirm the instructions."
2. For a company buyer, say **builder's risk insurance now and landlord insurance after**, not homeowners insurance.
3. Remove the $150 title search credit; Green Abstract asked Jaz to stop promising it.
4. Never put "wire instructions" in a subject line.

## For Jaz to check

Nothing in email confirms "$503 a unit at 7%"; the only rate found is the 9.375% refinance offer. Check against your own records before the course goes out.
