# Email templates

These are starting points. Kim will send her real emails to replace them; when she does, use her wording word for word and keep only the facts in brackets as fill-ins. Every email is a draft. Copy the agent on this file and any team member Kim switched on. Sign each one with the sign-off in `references/settings.md`.

Never put "wire instructions" in an email subject to a title company. Scammers watch for it.

## Day one

**Title order** → the title company's order email, copy the closer
Subject: `[Address] – Title Order`
> Hello! Attached is the signed Agreement of Sale for a new title order.
> Agent: [agent name] — [agent email]
> Coordinator: Kim Gladden, Gladden Closings — [Kim's email]
> Buyer: [names] — [emails]
> Seller: [names]
> Other agent: [name, brokerage] — [email]
> Lender: [lender] — [loan officer email]
> Please copy me and [agent] on all communication.

**Deposit instructions request** → the title clerk and the buyer
Subject: `[Address] ([Client last name])`
> Hi [clerk first name], I hope you're doing well! The title order is in for [buyer], copied here, and we'd like the instructions for the deposit.
> Property · buyer · seller · price · initial deposit [amount] (held by [title company]) · lender and loan type, or cash · settlement date
> Please send the instructions to [buyer first name] directly, and let me know if you need anything else.
> [Buyer first name], when the instructions arrive: Before you send any money, call the title company at a number you found yourself to confirm the instructions. This is a standard step to protect you from wire fraud.

**Lender introduction** (financed files) → loan officer, copy processor
Subject: `[Address] ([Buyer]) is under contract`
> Hi [first name], [buyer] is officially under contract on [address]; the signed Agreement of Sale is attached.
> - Mortgage application: [date]
> - Commitment: [date]
> - Settlement: [date]
> Title is with [title company]. Please keep us copied and let us know once the application is complete and the appraisal is ordered.

**Key dates** → the other agent, copy their coordinator
Subject: `[Address] – Key dates`
> Hi [first name], looking forward to a smooth one together. Here are the key dates. Please confirm or let me know of any discrepancies:
> [execution, deposit, mortgage application, reply to inspection, commitment, settlement]
> Lender: [lender]. Title: [title company] — [order email]. Please copy me and [agent] on all correspondence.

**Welcome email** → the client
Subject: `[Address] – Congratulations, you're under contract!`
> Hi [first names], congratulations! Your fully signed Agreement of Sale for [address] is attached. Gladden Closings is coordinating your file with [agent], and here are the dates to keep in mind:
> [deposit, mortgage application, inspection and reply, commitment, settlement — plain words, no acronyms]
> Your deposit of [amount] is due by [date]. You'll receive instructions from [title company]. Before you send any money, call the title company at a number you found yourself to confirm the instructions.
> [Financed: please don't open new credit, change jobs or make large purchases before closing without talking to your lender.]
> Please reply all so everyone stays in the loop.

**Company documents** (LLC buyer or seller) → the client
> To keep title on schedule, please send the company's operating agreement, certificate of organization and tax ID (EIN) letter this week.

**Housing counselor check-in** (assistance program files) → the counselor Kim names for the file
> Hi [first name], I hope you're doing well! Our mutual client, [client], is now under contract on [address], and I wanted to check in on what's needed to get their file submitted. Please let me know at your earliest convenience.

## When documents come in

**Mortgage commitment** → the listing agent, copy their coordinator; attach only the commitment letter
Subject: `[Address] – Mortgage Commitment`
> Hi [first name], good news! Attached is [buyer]'s mortgage commitment for [address]. We're on track for settlement on [date]. Please let me know if your seller needs anything else from our side.

**Schedule settlement** → title closer
> Hi [first name], [buyer] is clear to close on [address]. Can we schedule settlement for [date] at [time, from settings]? Please send the settlement notice once it's set.

**Settlement details** → the client
> Hi [first name], settlement is set for [day, date] at [time] at [place]. Please bring a photo ID. Your final amount will come from [title company]. Before you send any money, call the title company at a number you found yourself to confirm the instructions.

## Follow-ups (deadline sweep)

- **Lender, early:** "Has the application been completed, and has the appraisal been ordered? If the appraisal is scheduled, please let me know the date."
- **Title:** "Please let us know when the deposit is received."
- **Buyer, deposit:** "Kindly forward the bank's wire confirmation for your deposit once available. Before you send any money, call the title company at a number you found yourself to confirm the instructions."
- **Lender, late:** "Do we have an expected date on the mortgage commitment? Is anything outstanding for clear to close?"
- **Lender, 3 business days out:** "Please make sure the closing disclosure is signed at least three business days before settlement."
- **Buyer, insurance:** "Settlement is [date]. Please send your homeowners insurance binder to your lender and copy me."
- **Closing day, to title:** "Happy closing day! Once closing is complete, may we have a copy of the final signed documents?"
