# Jasmine's effects (BuySmart Philly)

Her own showcase effects, made for her and not in the kit's library: the Philly physical world (row homes, brick,
a front door, a hanging sign, keys) and her brand's own marks (The Dot, the stepper). Every colour and font is a
token from her style pack, so they come out in her look. They follow the kit's contract (`kit/FX_BRIEF.md`) and
are used like the kit's own effects: copy a folder to `<project>/fx/<id>/`, change the constants at the top of
`fx.js` (or run the generator named in the row), and add its edit.FX entry. Call them by name in a request
("dot-route as the motif, full-stop on the first line") or in a storyboard note. `preview.jpg` is a frame from the
demo on the drawn stand-in figure; masks and GEN lines in these folders are the demo's and are rebuilt per video by
their generators.
